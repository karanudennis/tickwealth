Questions

What is paper (ghost) trading?

Trades use live Deriv prices and the payout Deriv quotes, but with virtual money. They settle on real ticks, so you see realistic wins and losses without risking anything.

How do I trade with my Deriv account?

Tap the account button, then Log in with Deriv, or paste an API token with Read and Trade scopes. Use a demo account first. Real-money trades always ask you to confirm.

What do the percentages mean?

They show how far an outcome's recent frequency is above its normal probability. Green is 70% and up, yellow 55–69%, red below that. They describe the past only.

Do the bots guarantee profit?

No. Bots follow fixed rules on live ticks and can lose. They start in paper mode, stop at your stop-loss, and martingale is off unless you switch it on.

Does the AI coach place trades?

Never. It explains numbers, reviews your session and talks through risk. Every trade is your decision.

Is this free?

Practising in paper mode needs no account and no payment.
